Tungnath-Chandrashila Trip (2024)

Reaching the highest Shiva temple in the world

Unlike my previous trips with school friends, this time, I set off with my university friends for an adventurous trek to Tungnath and Chandrashila Peak. It was a different group, a different energy, and a different kind of thrill.

🚶‍♂ The Trek from Chopta to Tungnath

The journey began from Chopta, a small yet stunning hill station known as the "Mini Switzerland of India." The 5 km trek to Tungnath, the highest Shiva temple in the world, was challenging but breathtaking. We walked through dense forests of rhododendrons, open meadows, and steep rocky paths, with occasional patches of snow.

Our group was full of enthusiasm, stopping to take pictures, cracking jokes, and competing to see who could reach first. When we finally arrived at Tungnath Temple, standing tall at 12,000 ft, we felt a deep sense of accomplishment.

🏔 Reaching Chandrashila Peak – Above the Clouds

The real challenge began after Tungnath—the climb to Chandrashila Peak (13,000 ft). The trail was steeper, the air thinner, and the wind stronger. But as we pushed ourselves to the top, the reward was beyond imagination.

Standing at the summit, we were surrounded by a 360-degree view of the Himalayas—Nanda Devi, Trishul, Chaukhamba, and Kedar Dome all stood in their majestic glory. It was like touching the sky.

At that moment, we all sat in silence, taking in the beauty, feeling the cold Himalayan wind, and realizing that some experiences in life can't be captured in words or pictures—they can only be felt.

This trek was not just about adventure—it was about pushing limits, making new friendships stronger, and witnessing nature at its absolute best.
