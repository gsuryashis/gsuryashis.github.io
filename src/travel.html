Travel Adventures

I love trekking and exploring new places every year. Traveling brings me closer to nature, history, and adventure. Here are some of my recent journeys:

🏝 Vizag-Araku (2022)

In 2022, I explored the beautiful coastal city of Visakhapatnam (Vizag) and the mesmerizing Araku Valley. The scenic train ride through the lush green valleys, the Borra Caves, and the coffee plantations made this trip unforgettable.

A spiritual and adventurous journey to the Kedarnath and Badrinath temples in 2023 was truly special. The breathtaking trek to Kedarnath, the divine atmosphere, and the snow-capped peaks made it a surreal experience.

In 2024, I hiked to Tungnath, the highest Shiva temple in the world, and further to Chandrashila Peak. The panoramic view of the Himalayas from the top was one of the most rewarding sights of my life.
