Research Projects & Internships

The Returns to Hiding Identity: A Field Experiment in India

Summer, 2026
Supervisors: Prof. Arkadev Ghosh (Duke University), Prof. Anujit Chakraborty (University of California), Prof. Matt Lowe (University of British Columbia), Prof. Gareth Nellis (University of California)

Assisted in implementing a randomized field experiment on caste concealment and labor market outcomes in India. Collected and analyzed data from 1,000+ participants, executed daily treatment randomization in R, and conducted routine data quality checks to ensure protocol compliance.

Cyclical Dynamics of R&D Composition in U.S. Firms

Spring, 2025
Supervisor: Prof. Assa Cohen (Sy Syms School of Business, Yeshiva University, U.S.)

Assisted in empirical analysis of a semi-endogenous growth model studying cyclical shifts between basic and applied R&D. Cleaned and merged BRDIS/SIRD microdata (1985–2021) covering 10,000+ firm-year observations and supported preliminary regression analysis in Stata and R.

Effect of Health & Wellness Centers under Ayushman Bharat on Out Of Pocket Expenditure of Geriatric Population: A Multicenter Study

Summer, 2024
Supervisor: Prof. Indranil Mukhopadhyay (Jindal School of Government & Public Policy, O.P. Jindal Global University, India)

Assisted in evaluating the impact of Health & Wellness Centers under Ayushman Bharat on geriatric out-of-pocket healthcare expenditure across Rajasthan, Tripura, and Karnataka. Analyzed survey data from 9,000+ individuals using descriptive statistics and regression analysis in Stata.

Academic Projects

Cost of Holding Foreign Exchange Reserves in India (M.A. Dissertation)

Aug 2025–Present
Supervisors: Prof. Prachi Mishra (Ashoka University), Adrian Peralta-Alva (IMF)

Analyzing whether India’s foreign exchange reserves exceed economically optimal levels and assessing the cost of holding excess reserves in low-return assets. Examining the scope for reallocating surplus reserves toward more productive uses without compromising external stability.

Rainfall Shocks and Birth Spacing in India: Evidence of Precautionary Fertility and Son Preference

Nov 2025
Supervisor: Prof. Bipasha Maity (Ashoka University, India)

Analyzing how district-level rainfall shocks affect birth spacing decisions in India and assessing whether fertility responses differ based on prior son composition. Examining the interaction between income uncertainty, precautionary fertility behavior, and entrenched gender preferences.

Credit Default Risk Modeling and Visualization Dashboard (Machine Learning & Python)

Apr 2025
Supervisor: Kush Khurana (Ashoka University, India)

Analyzing borrower-level credit risk using supervised machine learning models and assessing predictive performance across alternative classification frameworks. Designing an interactive dashboard to visualize default probabilities and borrower risk profiles for real-time decision support.
